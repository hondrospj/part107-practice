Try answering cards aloud before revealing the back. A correct guess still needs review. Cards you mark “Got it” return after one day, then three, then seven; “Need practice” keeps them due.

Seven hours is a first preparation cycle. Reserve an additional uninterrupted two-hour block for a fresh full practice exam. Use the results to decide whether you need more study. An 85% practice target is a preparation goal, not an FAA rule or a passing guarantee.

The current exam has 60 scored questions and five unscored questions, with two hours total and a 70% passing score. The lessons emphasize regulations, operations and airspace, while including weather and performance.
