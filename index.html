How this practice exam works

Each form has 60 scored questions and five unscored practice questions. You won’t see which five are unscored until you submit. 42 of the 60 scored questions must be correct to reach 70%.

Questions and answer choices are shuffled. Answers, flags, and the timer are saved in this browser. The clock keeps running if you leave the tab. Explanations stay hidden until you finish.

The scored questions follow PSI’s current topic weighting, rounded to whole questions: regulations 29, airspace 12, weather 3, loading and performance 1, operations 15. These are original practice questions, not an official FAA exam or a guarantee of your test-day score.

Your answers stay on this device. GitHub does not receive your score. After finishing, use Copy results to share your score in this chat.

What you need for the real test
  1. Create an IACRA account and get your FAA Tracking Number (FTN).
  2. Book Unmanned Aircraft General – Small (UAG) through PSI. Search ZIP 08260 for locations and appointments. A same-day appointment is not guaranteed.
  3. Bring acceptable current ID and any required proof of your residential address. Check PSI’s confirmation before going.
  4. Budget about $175 for the exam; confirm the amount when booking. No required prep course or instructor endorsement is needed.

The exam can be taken from age 14; the remote pilot certificate requires age 16. After a failed test, there is a 14-day waiting period before a retake. Passing the test is followed by an IACRA certificate application and TSA review.

From October 26, 2026, UAG questions can use chart images absent from the printed supplement. Practice reading the chart, not memorizing an image.
